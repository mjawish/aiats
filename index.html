About AIATS

AIATS is an open engineering initiative focused on algorithmic trading, systematic investing, quantitative research, and intelligent market automation.

Our mission is to bridge financial markets and software engineering by developing transparent, reproducible, and scalable trading systems.

Indicators, strategies, signal generation, and Pine Script development.

Algorithmic Trading

Research, backtesting, optimization, and systematic trading workflows.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, predictive analytics, and AI-assisted trading systems.
